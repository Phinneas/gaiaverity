We've rebuilt GaiaVerity from the ground up, and we're excited to finally share what we've been working on.

If you've visited before, you might notice things feel a bit different. That's intentional. The old site served its purpose, but as our content grew and our audience grew with it, we needed something that could keep up — and something that wouldn't make you wait around while it loaded.

The new GaiaVerity is built on a modern, fast-loading foundation. Pages are snappier, navigation is cleaner, and everything is organized to help you find what you're looking for without digging. Whether you're troubleshooting mushrooms in your lawn, figuring out what ground cover can survive a dry Pacific Northwest summer, or planning a vertical garden on a small fence line — you should be able to get to the answer quickly and get back outside.

### What's new

**Significantly faster load times**  
Built on Astro — pages load near-instantly so you spend less time waiting and more time growing.

**Cleaner reading experience**  
Less clutter, better typography, and content that's easier to scan on any device.

**Easier to maintain and grow**  
A leaner back end means we can publish better content faster and keep everything up to date.
